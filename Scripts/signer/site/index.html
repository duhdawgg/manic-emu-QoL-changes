If an app won't install or open
  1. Tap Install in Safari (not inside another app), then tap Install again on the pop-up. It downloads on your Home Screen.
  2. First app only (development signing): open Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode, turn it on, restart, and tap Turn On after the restart.
  3. "Unable to install": your iPhone isn't in the profile. Check the DEVICE_UDID secret and sign the app again.
  4. Apps stop opening when the certificate expires (about a year). Just sign them again.
